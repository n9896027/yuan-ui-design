## Verified connector facts

- [Raspberry Pi 4 Model B official specifications](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/specifications/), accessed 2026-11-24: USB-C is 5 V DC input (minimum 3 A); the board has two micro-HDMI ports (up to 4Kp60).

## Explicitly non-factual example material

The three-hole enclosure, visual positions in the WebGL drawing, cable/blanking-plug options, and compatibility rules are fictional local example data. They are neither official CAD nor measured mechanical compatibility advice. The WebGL verification only proves a browser context drew the schematic in the tested environment; it does not identify GPU hardware or verify a physical enclosure.

## Hardware facts used for the annotations

- [Raspberry Pi 4 Model B — official specifications](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/specifications/), accessed 2026-11-24.
  - USB-C: 5 V DC input, minimum 3 A.
  - Video: two micro-HDMI ports, up to 4Kp60.

The photo visibly identifies `POWER IN`, `HDMI0`, and `HDMI1`; the official specification verifies the connector type and capability. This sample deliberately does not infer unshown ports or the exact board revision from the crop.
